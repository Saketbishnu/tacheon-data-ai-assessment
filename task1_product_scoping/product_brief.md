# Product Brief — Marketing Performance Intelligence Tool

## Problem Statement

Marketing teams and clients repeatedly ask:

"How is our marketing performing across channels right now, and where should we focus?"

Currently, answering this question requires manual effort:

 Analysts log into multiple platforms
 Data is collected manually
 Numbers are combined differently depending on who performs the work
 Response times are inconsistent

This creates inefficiency, inconsistency, and dependency on specific team members.


## Product Goal

Build an internal tool that helps marketing teams quickly understand:

1. Current cross-channel marketing performance
2. Best-performing channels
3. Channels requiring attention
4. Recommended focus areas

The tool should reduce manual work while fitting into existing workflows.


## Primary Users

### Primary User

Internal Marketing Analyst

Needs:

 Fast reporting
 Reliable numbers
 Consistent performance summaries
 Reduced manual work

### Secondary User

Client-facing account manager

Needs:

 Easy-to-understand insights
 Client-ready summaries
 Confidence in reported metrics


## Success Criteria

A successful interaction should allow a user to:

View marketing performance across channels
Identify strongest and weakest channels
Understand where to focus next
Generate consistent performance summaries quickly
